COMMON QUESTIONS ANSWERED
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Why can't users login?
A: Token key mismatch - token stored as "authToken" but retrieved as "dc_token"
   FIX: Change api.js lines 11 and 21

Q: Why is JWT verification failing?
A: JWT_SECRET not defined in backend/.env
   FIX: Add JWT_SECRET=... to backend/.env

Q: Why are weak passwords accepted?
A: Validation mismatch - registerDevValidation doesn't use password strength rules
   FIX: Use passwordStrengthValidation in registerDevValidation

Q: Why are password errors cryptic?
A: No error handling around password verification
   FIX: Add try-catch around matchPassword()

Q: Why are some users locked out?
A: No validation that password was hashed during registration
   FIX: Check password field after User.create()

Q: When will this be production-ready?
A: After fixing all 6 documented errors
